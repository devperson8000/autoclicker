Easy Autoclicker (Bookmarklet)

Drag the button below to your bookmarks bar. No install, no admin permissions needed.

🖱️ Easy Autoclicker

Setup

  1. Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (Cmd+Shift+B in Chrome/Safari).
  2. Drag the purple 🖱️ Easy Autoclicker button above onto your bookmarks bar.
  3. Go to any normal webpage.
  4. Click the bookmark. A small control panel appears in the top-right corner of the page.

If dragging doesn't work

Some managed browsers block drag-and-drop of bookmarks too. If so, add it manually instead:

  1. Right-click your bookmarks bar → Add Page (or New Bookmark).
  2. Name it "Easy Autoclicker".
  3. For the URL, open bookmarklet-uri.txt in this folder, copy its entire contents, and paste it as the URL.
  4. Save. Click it on any webpage to open the panel.

Using it

  • Set CPS in the panel.
  • Always On: click Start (or press the hotkey, default F6) to click continuously wherever your mouse is; click Stop / press the hotkey again to end.
  • Hold Key: check "Enable hold-to-click", then hold the hotkey down — it clicks while held, stops on release.
  • Click the hotkey button in the panel, then press any key, to change it.
  • Drag the panel by its top bar to move it. Click the × to close it.

Limitations

  • You have to click the bookmark again each time you load/refresh a page — it doesn't persist like an extension.
  • Only clicks elements on the current page; it can't click outside the browser window.
  • Some sites (canvas-based games, strict anti-bot checks) may not respond to script-simulated clicks.
